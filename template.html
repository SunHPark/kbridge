KEV Camp Guide: live a week in a hanok in Gimhae, Korea

K-Bridge International's KEV camp is a 4-night, 5-day Korean language and culture program in a traditional hanok in Gimhae, next to Busan. Korean class, K-POP dance, K-food, K-beauty and a Gimhae day tour are included. No Korean needed.
